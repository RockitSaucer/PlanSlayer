PlanSlayer V1.3.38 — Got it! → Drop + claimed glow keeps member colors
======================================================================
Date: 2026-08-10

Shell: plan-slayer-shell-v71

Got it! / Drop
--------------
- When an item is fully gotten AND you claimed any of it, the Got it! button
  swaps to red Drop.
- Drop clears only your claims and runs the same claim-sync path (My checklist /
  Personal list / buy→bring) with qty 0 so the item goes back on the list.

Claimed look
------------
- Fully grabbed items no longer replace member colors with a flat green tile.
- claimFaceStyle always paints member claim bands.
- is-claimed (any claims) and is-full (fully gotten) add a brighter glow so
  grabbed rows stand out while member colors stay visible.
