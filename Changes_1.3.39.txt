Dictate (#51)
-------------
- Button shows **Stop listening** while active (not only “Listening…”).
- Hard-stop: stop() then abort() fallback; always clear recognition handle.
- Max listen ~40s auto-stops with toast.
- onerror toasts short reason (mic denied / no speech / network).
- Continuous multi-item split into lines unchanged.

Photo draft auto-save (#52)
---------------------------
- While OCR review is open with any lines, tab close/hide flushes:
  - Prefer auto-apply into the open list/note (same path as Add to list).
  - Else park draft in localStorage and restore on next load.
- Empty reviews are not saved.
- Toast: “Saved photo items from last session” on restore/apply.
