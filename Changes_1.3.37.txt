PlanSlayer V1.3.37 — My checklist full item options
===================================================
Date: 2026-08-10

Shell: plan-slayer-shell-v70

Problem
-------
Items under “My checklist” did not open the same expand/options panel as
To do / To buy / To bring (clicking often did nothing).

Cause
-----
My checklist is built from private mirrors + personal-column copies whose
ids often differ from the source pack items. Click/save lookup only scanned
normal column buckets, so expand never found the row.

Fix
---
1. cloneItemOptionFields / enrichChecklistDisplayItem — checklist rows carry
   full option fields (title, qty, category, due, chore, highlight, notes…).
2. resolveNamedListItemHit — finds personal column, private store, or live
   claim; materializes private rows into the personal column when needed.
3. saveNamedListItemHit — persists checklist edits to private store + list.
4. Click, expand, Got it, category, and detail-save paths use the new resolver.
5. syncClaimToPrivateChecklist copies full option fields when Got it! mirrors.
